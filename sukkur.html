The Sajjad Experience in Sukkur

Carrying forward the legacy of Sajjad Restaurant, our Sukkur branch brings the same celebrated cuisine, warm hospitality, and unforgettable ambiance to the heart of Sindh.


From sizzling BBQ platters fired over open coals to traditional Pakistani favourites, every dish is crafted with the same care that has made Sajjad a household name. Our beautifully designed outdoor courtyards and family-friendly dining halls make every visit a memorable occasion — whether it's a quiet family dinner, a celebration, or an evening with friends under the Sukkur sky.
