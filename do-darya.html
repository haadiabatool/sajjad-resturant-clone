The Sajjad Experience

Sajjad Restaurant is one of Karachi's most iconic dining destinations located at Do Darya. Guests enjoy exceptional cuisine while overlooking the Arabian Sea in an unforgettable atmosphere.


Our beautifully designed chalets provide the perfect setting for family dinners, celebrations, and special occasions. With world-class hospitality, flavorful dishes, and stunning ocean views, Sajjad delivers a dining experience unlike any other.
